Am I eligible?

Most early-stage startups qualify. Here's what we look for.

Company Requirements

  • Company is less than 10 years old
  • Revenue under $100M annually
  • Not previously received AWS Activate credits exceeding program limit
  • Has a valid business website or product

Funding Stage

  • Activate Standard: Any stage (bootstrapped OK)
  • Activate Pro: Seed or higher, associated with a VC/accelerator
  • Growth Credits: Series A+
  • Migrate Credits: Any funded stage

Technical Requirements

  • Active AWS account (or create one when you apply)
  • Plan to use AWS for your primary workload
  • No minimum current AWS spend
  • Credits work with any AWS service

What to Expect

  • Application takes under 5 minutes
  • Most approvals are instant (Activate Standard)
  • Pro/Growth programs: response within 24 hours
  • Credits activated same day upon approval

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to talk to an AWS sales rep?
No. The entire process is self-service. You apply online, get approved automatically (for most programs), and credits are applied to your account — all without speaking to anyone.
How long do the credits last?
Activate Standard credits are valid for 12 months. Activate Pro and Growth Credits are valid for 24 months. Migrate Credits are valid for 12 months from activation.
Can I apply to multiple programs?
You can apply to one Activate program (Standard or Pro) plus one additional program (Growth or Migrate) if you meet the eligibility requirements for each.
What AWS services do credits cover?
Credits apply to most AWS services including EC2, S3, Lambda, RDS, Bedrock, SageMaker, and more. Some exclusions apply (e.g., AWS Marketplace purchases, Route 53 domain registration).
What happens when my credits run out?
You'll be billed at standard AWS pay-as-you-go rates for any usage beyond your credit balance. We'll notify you when your credits are running low so there are no surprises.
I was rejected. Can I reapply?
Yes. If your situation has changed (e.g., you've received funding, launched your product), you can reapply after 90 days. You'll be told why you were not approved so you know what to address.
