Frequently asked questions

Do I need to talk to an AWS sales rep?
No. The entire process is self-service. You apply online, get approved automatically (for most programs), and credits are applied to your account — all without speaking to anyone.
How long do the credits last?
Credit validity varies by program, typically ranging from 12 to 24 months. You'll see the specific terms during the application process.
Can I apply to multiple programs?
Yes — you may be eligible for multiple programs depending on your use case. For example, you could qualify for both Activate and the Gen AI program if you're building AI applications.
What AWS services do credits cover?
Credits apply to most AWS services including EC2, S3, Lambda, RDS, Bedrock, SageMaker, and more. Some exclusions apply (e.g., AWS Marketplace purchases, Route 53 domain registration).
What happens when my credits run out?
You'll be billed at standard AWS pay-as-you-go rates for any usage beyond your credit balance. We'll notify you when your credits are running low so there are no surprises.
